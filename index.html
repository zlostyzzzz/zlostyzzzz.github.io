🎮 What Is gt-2-pc?

gt-2-pc is a complete re-imagining of the beloved Gran Turismo 2, rebuilt from the ground up in the C++ programming language. This isn't an emulator or a cloud stream – it's a native application that runs directly on your device's hardware. That means smoother gameplay, faster loading times, and the authentic feel of the original PlayStation classic.

Whether you're a long-time fan who remembers the iconic menu music, or a newcomer curious about racing history, gt-2-pc delivers the full experience: over 400 cars, 27 tracks, and that addictive GT Simulation mode that defined a generation of racing games.
